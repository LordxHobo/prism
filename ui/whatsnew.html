What's new in Prism

Version 0.4.0

Meet Prism Shields

Prism now blocks ads, trackers and malware on its own, with the same filter lists as uBlock Origin and Brave's blocking engine. It's on everywhere, no extension needed. Click the shield in the address bar to see what it blocked, or to turn it off for a site.

New in 0.4

  • Prism Shields: ad and tracker domains are refused before Prism connects to them, leftover ad boxes are hidden, and site-specific fixes from uBlock Origin defuse ads served from a site's own servers (YouTube's video ads, for example).
  • Ads from a site's own servers (like the side banners on hltv.org) are found by matching their images and frames against the filter lists, then hidden.
  • Shields panel on the address-bar shield: Shields on or off for this site, how much was blocked, and recently blocked domains with an Allow button for when something breaks. The shield turns grey on sites where Shields is off.
  • Settings → Shields (open): pick filter lists (cookie notices and other annoyances are optional), update them now, add your own filters, and manage sites and domains you've allowed.
  • Allow from the blocked page: if Shields blocks a site you opened yourself, you can allow it right there.
  • Extensions page: installs now show each step and the download progress, and extensions that block ads show Blocking on.

Good to know

  • Filter lists download the first time Prism starts (about 6 MB) and update every 4 days. Pages opened before they're ready aren't filtered yet.
  • Turning Shields off for a site stops hiding ads and site fixes there. Ad and tracker domains stay blocked everywhere until Prism has its own WebKit build (0.9); use Allow in the panel if a site needs one.
  • Changes to Shields apply to pages you load afterwards. The panel reloads the tab for you.
  • Extension popups, options pages and background tasks are next, in 0.5.

Earlier: 0.3

  • Extensions from the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons (prism://extensions, Ctrl Shift E), with a permissions check before every install. Content scripts, saved settings and filter-list ad blocking work.

Earlier: 0.2

  • Settings page with search engine choice, "Continue where you left off", download folder, theme, accent color, compact tabs, cookie blocking level, and clearing browsing data.

Earlier: 0.1, the first build

  • Tabs with a tab strip, middle-click to close, and reopen closed tabs.
  • Address bar that takes web addresses or searches (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Privacy on by default: WebKit's tracking prevention, third-party cookies blocked, no DNS prefetching, no link-click pings, and popups only when you click.
  • Zero telemetry. Prism has no analytics, crash reporting or accounts, and it never contacts us.
  • Downloads save to your Downloads folder.
  • Dark and light themes that follow your Windows setting.
  • Developer tools with F12.
