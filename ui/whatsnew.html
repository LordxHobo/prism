What's new in Prism

Version 0.2.0

Settings are here

Open them from the ⋯ menu, with Ctrl ,, or by clicking the green shield in the address bar.

New in 0.2

  • Settings page (prism://settings) with General, Appearance, Privacy and About sections. Changes apply immediately.
  • Search engine choice: DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave Search, Ecosia, Google, Bing, or your own.
  • Continue where you left off: reopen your windows and tabs when Prism starts.
  • Download folder of your choice.
  • Theme, accent color and compact tabs. Pick System, Dark or Light, one of eight accent colors, and a slimmer tab strip.
  • Third-party cookies: Standard or Strict blocking.
  • Clear browsing data now, or automatically every time Prism closes.
  • Opening What's new or Settings switches to the tab if it's already open.

Earlier: 0.1, the first build

  • Tabs with a tab strip, middle-click to close, and reopen closed tabs.
  • Address bar that takes web addresses or searches (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Privacy on by default: WebKit's tracking prevention, third-party cookies blocked, no DNS prefetching, no link-click pings, and popups only when you click.
  • Zero telemetry. Prism has no analytics, crash reporting or accounts, and it never contacts us.
  • Downloads save to your Downloads folder.
  • Dark and light themes that follow your Windows setting.
  • Developer tools with F12.
