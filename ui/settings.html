Tabs

Tab position

Vertical puts your tabs in a list on the left. The button at its top narrows it to icons.

Tab groups

Using tab groups

  • Right-click a tab and choose Add tab to new group. Right-click the group to name it, pick a color, collapse it or close it.
  • Drag tabs to reorder them. Drop a tab next to a grouped tab (or on a group) to add it to that group.
  • With two rows, Ctrl 1–9 picks a group as shown (the selected one is always first); pressing it again goes through that group's tabs. Drag a group to give it a new place.

Privacy

Always on

  • No telemetry. Prism never sends anything about you or your browsing to anyone.
  • Tracking prevention. Sites that follow you around the web lose access to their cookies and storage.
  • No link pings or DNS prefetching. Links can't report your clicks, and Prism doesn't look up links you didn't click.
  • Popups only when you click.
Third-party cookies

HTTPS

Takes click trackers like utm_source, fbclid and gclid out of addresses before pages open.

Sites can try to recognize your PC from how it draws pictures and sound and from its hardware. Prism makes those details slightly different for every site and every time you start it, so they can't follow you. Applies to pages opened afterwards.

      Shields

      Prism Shields blocks ads, trackers and malware on every site, using the same filter lists as uBlock Origin. It's built in: no extension needed.

      Filter lists

      Lists update on their own every few days.

      Your own filters

      One per line, in uBlock Origin / Adblock Plus syntax. For example ||ads.example.com^ blocks a domain and example.com##.banner hides an element.

      Sites with Shields off

      Turn Shields off for a site from the shield in the address bar.

        Domains you allowed

        Never blocked, even if a filter list says so. Allow one from the Shields panel's “Recently blocked” list.
