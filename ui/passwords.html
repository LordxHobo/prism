Prism keeps your logins in an encrypted vault on this computer and fills them in for you. Nothing is sent anywhere. Choose a master password to protect the vault.

At least 8 characters. A few random words make a strong one.

Prism then opens the vault by itself when you're signed in to this Windows account. You still need the master password to see, copy or export passwords. Turn this off to type the master password each time Prism starts.

If you forget the master password, nobody can get your passwords back, not even Prism.
