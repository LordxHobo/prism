What's new in Prism

Version 0.5.0

Extensions, for real this time

Extensions now run all their parts: the background part that works behind the scenes, their toolbar button and popup, and their options page. Dark Reader, uBlock Origin Lite's popup and similar extensions should work like in Chrome or Firefox.

New in 0.5

  • Toolbar buttons for extensions, next to the Extensions button, with their badges (like a count). Click for the popup; right-click for Options or Manage extension.
  • Background parts of extensions run, including Chrome's newer service workers, and they can talk to the pages they work on and to their popups.
  • Options pages open in a tab, from the right-click menu or the new Options button on the Extensions page.
  • More of the extension API: tabs, windows, toolbar buttons, running scripts in pages, alarms, and asking for extra permissions (Prism asks you first).
  • Privacy: an extension only sees which sites your tabs show if you allowed it to (its "tabs" or site permissions), or for the tab where you clicked its button.
  • Extensions page: each extension lists what doesn't work in Prism yet, instead of "Partly works" with no details.
  • Fixed: the shield's "blocked" counter stopped updating after a while.

Good to know

  • Not yet: right-click menu items from extensions, extension keyboard shortcuts, changing or blocking requests with webRequest, offscreen documents, and desktop notifications. Extensions that need these partly work.
  • Content scripts still run in the page itself (not a separate, protected copy like in Chrome), until Prism has its own WebKit build (0.9).
  • After turning an extension on or off, reload open tabs to apply it to them.
  • Bookmarks, history, find in page and printing are next, in 0.6.

Earlier: 0.4

  • Prism Shields, the built-in ad and tracker blocker (settings), with a panel on the address-bar shield.

Earlier: 0.3

  • Extensions from the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons (prism://extensions, Ctrl Shift E), with a permissions check before every install. Content scripts, saved settings and filter-list ad blocking work.

Earlier: 0.2

  • Settings page with search engine choice, "Continue where you left off", download folder, theme, accent color, compact tabs, cookie blocking level, and clearing browsing data.

Earlier: 0.1, the first build

  • Tabs with a tab strip, middle-click to close, and reopen closed tabs.
  • Address bar that takes web addresses or searches (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Privacy on by default: WebKit's tracking prevention, third-party cookies blocked, no DNS prefetching, no link-click pings, and popups only when you click.
  • Zero telemetry. Prism has no analytics, crash reporting or accounts, and it never contacts us.
  • Downloads save to your Downloads folder.
  • Dark and light themes that follow your Windows setting.
  • Developer tools with F12.
