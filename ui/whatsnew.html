What's new in Prism

Version 0.3.0

Extensions are here (early)

Install extensions from the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons: paste a link on the Extensions page, or press Add to Prism in the address bar on a store page. For ad blocking, try uBlock Origin Lite.

New in 0.3

  • Extensions page (prism://extensions, Ctrl Shift E, or the puzzle-piece button): install from a store link, a .crx/.xpi/.zip file, or an unpacked folder. Turn extensions on or off, or remove them.
  • Permissions check before every install, in plain language, plus what the extension can already do in Prism.
  • Content scripts work: extensions that change pages run on the sites they're made for, with saved settings (storage) and translations.
  • Ad and tracker blocking from filter-list extensions like uBlock Origin Lite. Prism blocks ad, tracker and malware domains before any connection is made, and you can turn individual filter lists on or off.
  • Blocked-site page: if you open a blocked site (like an ad link), Prism tells you which extension blocked it.
  • The shield in the address bar shows how many ads and trackers were blocked.

Good to know

  • Toolbar buttons, popups, options pages and background tasks of extensions come in 0.4, so some extensions only partly work for now. Each one's card shows what works.
  • Ad blocking works per domain for now. Ads served from the same domain as the video or page (like YouTube's own ads) aren't blocked yet.
  • After adding or turning on an extension, reload open tabs to use it there.

Earlier: 0.2

  • Settings page with search engine choice, "Continue where you left off", download folder, theme, accent color, compact tabs, cookie blocking level, and clearing browsing data.

Earlier: 0.1, the first build

  • Tabs with a tab strip, middle-click to close, and reopen closed tabs.
  • Address bar that takes web addresses or searches (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Privacy on by default: WebKit's tracking prevention, third-party cookies blocked, no DNS prefetching, no link-click pings, and popups only when you click.
  • Zero telemetry. Prism has no analytics, crash reporting or accounts, and it never contacts us.
  • Downloads save to your Downloads folder.
  • Dark and light themes that follow your Windows setting.
  • Developer tools with F12.
